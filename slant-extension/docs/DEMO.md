# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

**Before you start:** pair the browser, and open two YouTube tabs: one video already analyzed in Slant (for example Finance Bureau, "What Happens If Trump Loses The Midterms?") and one new, short video with captions.

1. **The idea (10 s).** "Slant shows how slanted a YouTube video is, with the quotes behind every score. On YouTube, it's just this bear."
2. **Instant result (20 s).** On the analyzed video, tap the bear. It changes colour and its bubble says **Tap me for results**. "Blue means it leans left, red right; the face reacts to sensationalism and unsupported claims."
3. **The card (25 s).** Tap the bear again. Walk down the card: the lean in big type (**−1.6**, a blue **Lean Left** pill) on the AllSides colour scale, then the five signals as "x / 10" bars. "The overall score, every quote and the transcript are one tap away." Tap **Full report ↗** to show the saved analysis on the Slant website, then come back and tap the bear (or **Close**) to hide the card.
4. **Receipts (20 s).** Click **Full report ↗**: the same analysis on the Slant website, with the quotes behind every score and the transcript. "Every quote is checked against the transcript; anything Slant can't find is hidden."
5. **Live analysis (35 s).** Switch to the new video and tap the bear. The speech bubble narrates the real steps with a seconds counter: checking the video, reading captions, analyzing, saving. "It's running on our backend in Mana, not in the browser. I can keep watching." When it finishes, the bear changes.
6. **Close (10 s).** "Paired, rate-limited, and the keys never leave the server. Same prompt, same scores as the web app."

**Fallbacks:** if the live analysis is slow (AI transcription can take a minute), keep talking over the steps, or switch to another analyzed video for an instant result.
