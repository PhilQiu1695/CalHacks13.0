# Slant app: current design notes

Exported from the app's metadata in Mana (`apps/slant/app`) on October 24, 2026. Implementation detail for `Slant.jsx`; the team design doc is [docs/DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md).

Slant UI. Transcript: sandbox daemon 'yt-bias' (/workspace/yt-bias/server.py, the Slant caption service, youtube-transcript-api) via useIngressUrl; falls back to Gemini YouTube-URL transcription through the Mana router when captions or the service are missing. Analysis: Mana router openai gpt-6-astra with a strict JSON schema (political lean on the AllSides Media Bias Meter scale -6..+6, five 0-10 signals (commercial interest removed 2026-10-24), overall shown as 'Slant score'; verbatim quotes with segment numbers, verified client-side against the transcript, and quotes that cannot be found are hidden). Results lead with political lean plus its AllSides label (on the score rounded to 0.1, AllSides bands: <=-3 Left, <=-1 Lean Left, <1 Center, <3 Lean Right, else Right). Pasting an already-analyzed video opens the saved report; Re-analyze re-runs it. Shorts (/shorts/ URLs) get a height-capped 9:16 player and portrait oardefault.jpg thumbnails. State: app/analyses/<id> (report), app/transcripts/<id>/snippets, and 'Disagree?' notes at app/analyses/<id>/flags/<uid> {part, note<=280 chars, at}; every flags node carries metadata/permissions {per_user_read, per_user_write} so any signed-in or anonymous visitor writes only their own note (new analyses get it at creation). View-only visitors are detected with a no-op delete probe at analyses/_probe_<uid>: they can open saved analyses but cannot start new ones, and the app says why before any credits are spent. URL params: ?slant=<id> opens a report, ?slant=how the How Slant works page. Mana credits are billed to the owner, including for shared viewers.
